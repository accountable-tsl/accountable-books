# Founder Story Talk Track (60–120 seconds)

Use this verbally. Keep the deck text shorter.

## Version A (tight, 60s)

"I’m a chartered accountant in Nigeria and the UK. I ran an accounting practice doing $100k+ in annual revenue, serving Nigerian companies and a few US/UK clients. Over time I realized our industry scales on people, not systems. So I went deep on tooling: I tried 38+ accounting and practice management products and got expert-level in 11 of them.

In 2024 I prototyped what I wanted in spreadsheets and hired a developer, but the double-entry and GAAP logic didn’t hold. That was the moment I realized: if you don’t have accounting depth, you can’t build accounting software. I shut down my practice, moved to the UK, did a software + AI engineering bootcamp, and then built Accountable Books end-to-end. Now I’m building the infrastructure I wish existed when I ran my firm: explainable automation that accountants can actually trust."

## Version B (advisor-first angle)

"I’m raising $500k to ship the close loop and onboard design partner firms. I’m also looking for investors who will be hands-on early: introductions to firms, credibility in the ecosystem, and help pressure-testing the go-to-market. This product is built for accounting reality, not demos, so the best partners are people who understand distribution and trust in the accounting ecosystem."

## What to avoid saying

- "I’m bad at sales."
- "I’m a recluse."
- "The developer was incompetent."

Better replacements

- "I’m product-led and I’m building a repeatable design-partner playbook; I’ll hire for channel/partnerships as pilots convert."
- "Accounting correctness is non-negotiable; we made the hard call to rebuild on a ledger-first foundation."
