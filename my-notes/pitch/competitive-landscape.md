# Competitive Landscape Notes (Working)

Purpose: keep a concise, defensible view of positioning for the deck.

## Categories

- AI-native bookkeeping/accounting: Kick, Digits, Puzzle, Rillet, Campfire, Accrual
- Legacy GL: QuickBooks Online, Xero
- Practice management (adjacent): Karbon, Keeper, TaxDome

## Quick reads (do not paste verbatim into deck)

### Kick

- Positioning: bookkeeping/tax product for business owners, with accountant collaboration.
- Deck angle: SMB-direct oriented; we are firm-first + multi-geo + audit/drill-first.

### Digits

- Positioning: accounting + finance (FP&A style) with AI and dashboards.
- Deck angle: strong UI/insights; we differentiate on firm operating model + close workflow + multi-geo.

### Puzzle

- Positioning: AI-enabled accounting for startups and accounting firms; markets "AI bookkeeping".
- Deck angle: nearest adjacent in "AI bookkeeping for firms"; differentiate on (1) multi-geo, (2) explicit close/workbench contract, (3) ledger mutation model + explainability posture.

### Rillet

- Positioning: accounting automation for mid-market, replaces manual close work; positions on speed and quality of close.
- Deck angle: mid-market focus; we start with firm-first portfolio throughput and expand up-market as controls mature.

### Campfire

- Positioning: modern accounting/ERP for mid-market, AI-first, broad suite.
- Deck angle: broad suite and enterprise posture; we are more focused wedge (categorization + close) with a modular expansion path.

### Accrual

- Positioning: AI platform for accounting firms (workflow + compliance/returns adjacent).
- Deck angle: strong firm workflow and AI; we differentiate by owning the accounting "truth layer" (ledger + drillable financials), not only workflows.

## Adjacent: Practice management tools

Karbon / Keeper / TaxDome are the "firm operating system" category:

- Great for tasks, CRM, pipelines, portals.
- Do not replace the accounting truth layer; usually integrate with QBO/Xero.

Deck framing:

- We can partner/integrate, but Accountable is building the accounting workbench and truth layer (and can later add firm workflow/communication primitives).
