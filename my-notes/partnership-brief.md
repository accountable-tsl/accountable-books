## The Problem

Nigeria has over 40 million micro, small, and medium enterprises. The vast majority manage their finances manually or with basic spreadsheets. The result:

- **No reliable financial records** — Businesses cannot produce accurate reports when they need credit, investment, or regulatory compliance
- **Compliance is an afterthought** — IFRS-for-SMEs adoption remains low because the tools don't exist to make it practical
- **Accountants are bottlenecked** — Firms spend most of their time on data entry and reconciliation, not advisory work
- **The gap is widening** — As regulatory expectations increase, the tools available to most businesses remain stuck in the past

This is not a Nigerian problem alone — it is a global one. But Nigeria, with its scale and regulatory momentum, is where we start.

## The Product

### Accountable Books (Current — In Development)

A cloud-based bookkeeping and accounting platform designed for two users:

1. **Accounting firms and freelancers** — who use it to serve their business clients efficiently
2. **SMEs** — who use it to self-serve their own bookkeeping and compliance

Core capabilities:

- Chart of accounts aligned to IFRS-for-SMEs
- Journal entry management (manual, recurring, and imported)
- Multi-period financial reporting
- Client management for firms serving multiple businesses
- Designed for the Nigerian regulatory context from day one

### A-One (Next Phase)

A suite of AI-enabled standalone modules that extend the platform such as payroll, vault, compliance, etc.

Each module can be purchased independently or as part of an integrated suite. This modular approach lets us serve businesses of all sizes — from a sole trader who only needs payroll, to a mid-size company that needs the full suite.

The long-term vision is an **Autonomous Finance OS** — software that doesn't just record transactions, but understands, categorizes, reconciles, and files them without little human intervention.

## Revenue Model

The business model deepens over time from pure SaaS to a hybrid of subscriptions and usage-based revenue:

| Stage     | Revenue Stream                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 2026      | SaaS subscriptions (per-firm, seat-based)      |
| Late 2026 | Payroll processing fees (per employee/month)   |
| 2027      | Tax remittance + automated filing fees         |
| 2027–28   | Payment rails (vendor pay, batch payouts)      |
| 2028+     | Full OS bundle: ~50% SaaS / ~50% usage revenue |

This progression from pure software to embedded financial rails is what creates a defensible, high-margin business at scale.

## The Partnership Opportunity

### What We Are Raising

We are raising **$300,000** via a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) in Accountable Techserve Inc. (the US parent entity).

**SAFE Terms:**

- **Instrument**: Post-money SAFE
- **Investment amount**: $300,000
- **Valuation cap**: $5,000,000
- **Discount**: 20% (standard)
- **Conversion**: Converts to equity at the next priced round at the lower of the cap or the discount

**What this means for you:**

- At a $5M cap with a $300K investment, you would convert to approximately 6% equity at the next priced round
- If the company raises at a higher valuation later, your cap protects your position
- The SAFE is the most founder-friendly and investor-efficient instrument for this stage — no liquidation preferences, or complex terms until a priced round
