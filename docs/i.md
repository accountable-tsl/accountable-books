| 16  | Financial Analysis     | Variance, trends, ratios                                   | Probabilistic (optional) | ✅ Optional         | Time-series, stats                   | Read-only insights. No postings.                    |

| 19  | Explanation Generation | Explain results, decisions, filings                        | Probabilistic            | ✅ Yes              | LLM (grounded), templates            | Must cite internal sources only.                    |
| 20  | Filing & Submission    | Generate returns, schedules, exports                       | Deterministic            | ❌ No               | —                                    | No AI in final numbers. Ever.                       |
